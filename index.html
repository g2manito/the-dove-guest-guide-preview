📺 Entertainment

Roku TVs use Guest Mode. Books, playing cards and a puzzle are available too.

🛏️ Bedrooms

Each bedroom has a charging station. Extra bedding is in the closets.

🌡️ Temperature

Ecobee is generally around 72°F cooling / 70°F heating.

🍽️ Microwave & septic

Press the LG microwave release button before pulling the door. Flush only toilet paper.

Enjoy

The best parts of staying in.

♨️ Private hot tub

The 7-person Hot Spring Limelight Pulse is on the lower deck and is generally set to 100°F. Maximum is 104°F.

  • Maximum 7 people
  • Children under 5 may not use the spa
  • Guests under 18 must be actively supervised
  • No glass, food or bath products
  • Rinse off sunscreen, lotions and oils first
  • Avoid use while impaired

Hot-tub towels: front entry closet.

If water looks unusually cloudy, discolored or foamy—or has a strong chemical odor—do not use the spa; contact your host.

🔥 Solo Stove

Use supplied firewood and starters. Keep the spark screen in place and never leave an active fire unattended.

🍔 Grill

Open lid and propane valve, ignite, cook, brush warm grates, turn burners off and close propane. Spare tank is in the garage.

⚡ EV charging

Tesla Universal Wall Connector in the garage supports Tesla and compatible non-Tesla EVs. Charging is complimentary.

Safety & house rules

Simple things to know.

Fire & CO

Combination smoke/CO alarms are installed in every bedroom and main living areas.

Severe weather

For a tornado warning, move to the interior hallway near the garage/laundry room, away from windows.

Outdoors

The backyard has slopes and uneven terrain. Supervise children. The nearby pond is viewing-only.

No smoking or vaping · No pets · Quiet hours 10 PM–8 AM · No parties or events · Registered overnight guests only.

Leave

Checkout is 10:00 AM.

There's no long chore list. Load and start the dishwasher; put used towels in the laundry-room hamper; remove personal food; tie trash and place it in the garage garbage can; turn off lights and fans; close exterior doors.

No need to strip beds, start laundry or change the thermostat.
