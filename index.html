Enjoy

The best parts of staying in.

♨️ Private hot tub

The 7-person Hot Spring Limelight Pulse is on the lower deck and is generally set to 100°F for arrival. Maximum temperature is 104°F.

  • Maximum 7 people
  • Children under 5 may not use the spa
  • Guests under 18 must be actively supervised
  • No glass, food or bath products
  • Rinse off sunscreen, lotion, oils and makeup first
  • Avoid use while impaired
  • Near 104°F, keep soaks brief—about 15–20 minutes

Hot-tub towels: front entry closet.

When finished, turn off jets and lights, replace and secure the cover, and bring towels inside.

If the water looks unusually cloudy, discolored or foamy—or has a strong chemical odor—do not use the spa; contact your host.

🔥 Solo Stove

Use supplied firewood and starters for a modest fire. Keep the spark screen in place and never leave an active fire unattended. Let flames burn down before placing the metal lid over the stove.

🍔 Grill

Open the lid and propane valve, ignite, cook, brush warm grates, turn burners off and close propane. A full spare tank is in the garage.

⚡ EV + outdoor utility

Tesla Universal Wall Connector in the garage supports Tesla and compatible non-Tesla EVs. Charging is complimentary. An outdoor hose is available at the front of the house, to the right of the front door.

Safety & house rules

Simple things to know.

Fire & CO

Combination smoke/CO alarms are installed in every bedroom and in the main/general living areas. Fire extinguishers are located in the laundry room, garage worktable area and upper deck.

Severe weather

For a tornado warning, move to the interior hallway near the garage/laundry room, away from windows and exterior doors.

Outdoors

The backyard has slopes and uneven terrain. Supervise children. The nearby pond is viewing-only.

  • No smoking or vaping anywhere on the property
  • No pets
  • Quiet hours: 10 PM–8 AM
  • No parties or events; small celebrations are welcome within occupancy, parking and quiet-hour limits
  • Primary booking guest must be at least 25
  • Only registered guests may stay overnight
Leave

Checkout is 10:00 AM.

There's no long chore list. We simply ask that you:

  1. Load and start the dishwasher.
  2. Place used towels in the laundry-room hamper.
  3. Remove personal food and beverages from the refrigerator, freezer and pantry.
  4. Tie trash bags and place them in the lidded garbage can in the garage.
  5. Turn off interior lights and ceiling fans.
  6. Make sure exterior doors are fully closed.

No need to strip beds, start laundry or change the thermostat.
