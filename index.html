Your private entry code is in your reservation arrival message. Exterior locks auto-lock after about five minutes.

Park up to four vehicles in The Dove driveway. Please stay off the grass and neighboring property.

Confirm you are using the current reservation code and that the door is fully closed. If access still fails, message or call the host using the contact information in your reservation. If there is an immediate safety emergency, call 911.
Settle in

Everything has a place.

🛏️ Bedrooms

Each bedroom has a bedside charging station. Extra blankets and pillows are stored in the closets.

🛁 Towels & toiletries

Eight guest towels are provided. Hot-tub towels are in the front-entry closet. Starter toiletries and hair dryers are available.

🧺 Laundry

Washer, dryer and liquid detergent are available. You’re welcome to wash towels during your stay.

🌡️ Comfort

The Ecobee is generally around 72°F cooling / 70°F heating. Adjust within the available range for comfort.

📺 Entertainment

Roku TVs use Guest Mode. Books are around the house; playing cards are in the dining room and a puzzle is under the TV console.

🚿 Septic

Please flush toilet paper only. Wipes and personal-care products belong in the trash—even when labeled “flushable.”

Quick how-to

The things guests usually ask about.

☕ Coffee

Choose the Nespresso VertuoPlus for pods or the Fellow Aiden for drip coffee. Starter coffee, tea, sweeteners and creamer are provided.

🍽️ LG microwave

Press the door-release/open button first, then pull the door open. Please don't force the door.

📺 Roku Guest Mode

Use Guest Mode for your streaming accounts and select your checkout date. Roku will automatically sign you out.

🍔 Gas grill

Open the lid before lighting. Open propane, ignite, cook, brush the warm grates, turn burners off, then close propane. A full spare tank is in the garage.

⚡ EV charging

The Tesla Universal Wall Connector in the garage supports Tesla and compatible non-Tesla EVs. Guest charging is complimentary.

💧 Outdoor hose

An outdoor hose is available at the front of the house, to the right of the front door.

Enjoy

The best parts of staying in.

Guest favorite

♨️ Hot Spring Limelight Pulse

The 7-person spa is on the lower deck and is generally prepared around 100°F. Maximum is 104°F.

  • Maximum 7 people
  • Children under 5 may not use the spa
  • Guests under 18 require active adult supervision
  • No glass, food or bath products
  • Rinse off sunscreen, lotions, oils and makeup first
  • Avoid use while impaired
  • At high temperatures, keep sessions short and exit if you feel unwell

When finished: turn off jets/lights, replace and secure the cover, and bring towels inside.

Cloudy, discolored or unusually foamy water—or a strong chemical odor? Don't use the spa; contact the host.

Safety & house rules

Know this before you need it.

🚨 Fire & CO

Combination smoke/CO alarms are installed in every bedroom and main living areas. Fire extinguishers are in the laundry room, garage worktable area and upper deck.

⛈️ Severe weather

For a tornado warning, move to the interior hallway near the garage/laundry room, away from windows and exterior doors.

🌲 Outdoors

The backyard has slopes and uneven terrain. Supervise children. The nearby pond is viewing-only.

  • No smoking or vaping anywhere on the property
  • No pets
  • Quiet hours: 10 PM–8 AM
  • No parties or events; small celebrations must remain within occupancy, parking and quiet-hour limits
  • Primary booking guest must be at least 25
  • Only registered guests may stay overnight
Leave

Checkout is 10:00 AM.

No long chore list. We simply ask that you:

  1. Load and start the dishwasher.
  2. Place used towels in the laundry-room hamper.
  3. Remove personal food and beverages from the refrigerator, freezer and pantry.
  4. Tie trash bags and place them in the lidded garbage can in the garage.
  5. Turn off interior lights and ceiling fans.
  6. Make sure exterior doors are fully closed.

No need to strip beds, start laundry or change the thermostat.
